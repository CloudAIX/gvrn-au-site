Your privacy policy needs an automated decisions section. Draft it in two minutes.

From 10 December 2026, organisations covered by the Australian Privacy Principles must disclose automated decisions that significantly affect people — including rule-based scripts and spreadsheets, not just AI. The OAIC began proactive privacy-policy sweeps in January 2026. Answer six questions; get draft wording you can hand to whoever owns your privacy policy.

1. Organisation name (as it appears in your privacy policy)

2. Which kinds of decisions does your organisation make using automated systems?

Includes decisions substantially assisted by automation, and rule-based systems — a spreadsheet formula that decides counts.

3. Which kinds of personal information do those systems use?

4. Is a human involved before decisions take effect?

5. Does any of this use AI or machine learning (as opposed to fixed rules only)?

6. Can people ask you to review an automated decision?

Not strictly required by the disclosure obligation — but offering review is where OAIC guidance and good practice are heading.
